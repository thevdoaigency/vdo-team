The Situation

We already have the hard part.

Real history. Real capabilities. Real customers. Many of our companies grew up inside or alongside Leopardo Construction — that gave us credibility. The gap: that proof isn't visible, organized, or being used consistently in the market.

This session is about closing that gap — helping these companies leave the nest and win more third-party customers.
